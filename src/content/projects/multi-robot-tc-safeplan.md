## Problem

When multiple robots act together, safety depends on more than each individual robot's local plan. Teams must coordinate observations, intentions, and constraints while communication can be delayed or incomplete.

## Motivation

This direction explores how semantic safety signals can be shared and used by multi-robot planners without assuming perfect communication or centralized access to every observation.

## Method

The planned work connects distributed perception, task execution, and risk-aware coordination. It is an ongoing research direction and does not yet claim a completed system or accepted venue.

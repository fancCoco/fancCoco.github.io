## Problem

Visual anomalies are not equally important for every task. A system needs to distinguish unusual observations from observations that meaningfully change the safety or feasibility of the current mission.

## Motivation

Task-aware anomaly detection can provide a more useful interface between perception and planning than a task-independent novelty score.

## Method

This project direction investigates semantic anomaly signals that can be evaluated alongside task constraints and downstream planning decisions. Specific datasets, experiments, and benchmarks will be added when available.

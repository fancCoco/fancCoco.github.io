## Problem

UAV planners must balance progress toward a task with uncertainty about the environment and their own models. A plan that is efficient under nominal assumptions may be brittle when those assumptions fail.

## Motivation

Risk-aware planning makes the cost of uncertainty explicit and creates room for graceful behavior when confidence is low.

## Method

This planned direction will study robust and risk-aware planning strategies for autonomous aerial systems. It is presented as a future research direction, without implying completed experiments or published results.
